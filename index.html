The problem

A commercial solar power purchase agreement says how energy should be billed and paid. Each month there is a meter reading, an invoice, and a payment. Those three are supposed to agree.

Sometimes they do not. The rate is wrong, or a yearly price increase in the contract is missed. The energy on the invoice does not match the meter. A month goes by with no invoice. A payment is short, or it shows up late.

The invoice, the meter file, and the contract are often kept apart, so a mismatch is easy to miss.

How it works

Three checks. The first two are version 1, for fixed-rate PPAs with monthly settlement.

  1. 01

    Contract check

    The billed rate, the escalator (the yearly price increase), and the other terms match the PPA.

  2. 02

    Meter check

    The billed kWh match the meter readings, and payments were made in full and on time.

  3. 03

    Weather check Coming soon

    Not available yet. Not part of version 1 or the free pilot.

    Given the actual weather, was metered production what it should have been?

    Weather is an independent record neither the seller nor the buyer controls, so it can show whether a lower month was cloudy or points to a meter or billing problem. The name WeatherAdjusted Solar refers to this check.

What version 1 covers

Version 1 is the contract check and the meter check, for fixed-rate PPAs with monthly settlement only.

Covered now

  • A fixed rate, including the escalator written into the contract
  • Monthly settlement
  • Billed kWh compared with meter readings, including a month that was not billed
  • Payments that are short, late, or missing

Not in version 1

  • Rates that change with the time of day, a market price, or a utility tariff
  • Any settlement schedule other than monthly
  • The weather check

If something you send is outside version 1, we will say so.
